## 🚀 Hosting Options

### GitHub Pages — Recommended

1. Request your preferred subdomain through Discord, Messenger, or email.
2. Open your portfolio repository on GitHub.
3. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**.
4. Under **Custom domain**, enter your assigned subdomain:

   ```text
   www.yourname.citcs.site
   ```

5. Click **Save**.
6. Wait for the green **“DNS check successful”** message.
7. Enable **Enforce HTTPS**.

> 💡 If **Enforce HTTPS** is unavailable, wait a few minutes and refresh the page. DNS and certificate setup can take some time. [1][3]

### Netlify

1. Deploy your website to Netlify.
2. Send me:
   - Your Netlify site link
   - Your preferred subdomain

Example:

```text
Netlify link: [https://my-portfolio.netlify.app](https://my-portfolio.netlify.app)
Preferred subdomain: www.juan.citcs.site
```

I’ll handle the domain connection from there.

### Vercel

1. Open your project in Vercel.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Domains**.
3. Click **Add** and enter your assigned subdomain:
4. Send me CNAME and TXT

   ```text
   www.yourname.citcs.site
   ```

5. Send me a message if you need help with verification or DNS settings.

Vercel supports adding custom domains through its dashboard or CLI. [10][11]
